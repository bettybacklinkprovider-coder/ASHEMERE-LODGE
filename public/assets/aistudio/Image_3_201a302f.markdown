## Brand & Style

The design system embodies "quiet luxury" and architectural minimalism within a cinematic, night-time environment. Tailored for ultra-high-net-worth patrons, architectural connoisseurs, and prospective residents of prime estates, the interface avoids aggressive commercial tactics in favor of contemplative reverence, space, and composure.

The visual expression converges two design paradigms:
- **Atmospheric Minimalism:** Vast negative space, disciplined structural alignment, and strict content restraint evoke the stillness of walking through a secluded woodland pavilion at twilight.
- **Subtle Glassmorphism & Tonal Restraint:** Translucent, deep-tinted surfaces soften background photography without interrupting spatial continuity. Fine-milled translucent borders reflect peripheral light like precision-cut architectural glazing.

Interactions are calibrated for slow grace: lingering transitions, silent state changes, and wide typographic tracking create an uncompromising editorial aura reminiscent of bespoke monographs and rare art folios.

## Colors

The palette derives from charred timber, architectural glass, slate, and glowing interior amber hearths.

### Color Tiers & Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#0F0F0F`)**: The deepest obsidian backdrop. Absorbs ambient light and grounds the photographic assets.
- **Surface Mid (`#141414`)**: Used for contextual panels, side navigation trays, and elevated layers.
- **Surface High (`#181818`)**: Interactive panels, elevated cards, and floating architectural index overlays.
- **Primary Accent (`#F2B36B`)**: Warm glowing amber. Used strictly for focal interactions, active timeline indicators, curated metadata pips, and deliberate luxury accents. Never over-saturated or used across broad structural fills.
- **Secondary (`#CFCFCF`)**: Muted bone off-white. Dedicated to secondary body titles, metadata readouts, architectural drawing line art, and structural navigation links.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F0F0F`)**: The tonal foundation.
- **Typography & Tonal Contrast**:
  - Primary Content: `#FFFFFF` (pure ambient light, reserved for prominent headlines and hero statements).
  - Secondary Content: `#CFCFCF` (bone white, for high-legibility narrative paragraphs).
  - De-emphasized Content: `#9E9E9E` (weathered stone, for legal text, technical specifications, and metadata markers).
  - Delimiting Borders: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(242, 179, 107, 0.20)` for interactive states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary authority of **Newsreader** (used for high-impact editorial statements, section introductions, and evocative headings) with the structural poise of **Outfit** (a pristine, light geometric sans-serif delivering effortless legibility and modernism).

### Typographic Rules
- **Headline Display & Editorial Headers (`Newsreader`)**: Rendered in low-weight, high-scale glyphs (`300` weight) with tight line heights to simulate physical architectural monographs.
- **Labels, Indexes & Navigation (`Outfit`)**: Rendered with extended letter-spacing (`0.18em` to `0.26em`) and uppercase transformation. This architectural labeling standard lends technical rigor to dates, coordinates, and spatial dimensions.
- **Body Narratives (`Outfit`)**: Kept at light weight (`300`) with generous line heights (`1.7`–`1.8x`) to ensure visual airiness against deep dark backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a classic 12-column editorial grid inspired by Swiss modernism and high-end print folios. Negative space acts as a structural material, prioritizing intentional voids over density.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Logic
- **Desktop (1440px+)**: 12-column layout. Margin: `4rem` (`space-xl` equivalent). Column Gutter: `2rem`. Max content wrapper: `1600px`. Asymmetric placement is encouraged (e.g., imagery spanning 7 columns, accompanying text occupying 3 columns with a 2-column offset).
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px)**: 8-column layout. Margin: `2.5rem`. Column Gutter: `1.5rem`. Multi-column narrative layouts reflow into stacked pairings.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout. Margin: `1.5rem`. Column Gutter: `1rem`. Hero elements fill horizontal edges with edge-to-edge photography and soft underlaid text scrollers.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section transitions mandate deliberate breath: vertical section margins consistently deploy `space-xl` or dynamic multiples (`6rem` to `10rem`), resisting dense cluster formations.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through optical density, surface luminance, and edge illumination rather than heavy dropshadows.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Base (`#0F0F0F`)**: Lowest physical foundation.
2. **Atmospheric Recesses**: High-resolution architectural imagery and landscape photography sit flush or slightly dimmed with dark directional vignette gradients (`rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.4)` to `#0F0F0F`).
3. **Glass Translucent Surfaces (Cards, Nav, Drawers)**:
   - Surface background: `rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.65)`.
   - Backdrop filter: `blur(20px) saturate(140%)`.
   - Perimeter highlight: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
4. **Interactive & Elevated Floating Shells**:
   - Surface background: `rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.85)`.
   - Backdrop filter: `blur(32px)`.
   - Perimeter highlight: `1px solid rgba(242, 179, 107, 0.18)` (subtle amber light catch).
   - Ambient Glow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 20px 0 rgba(242, 179, 107, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The architectural vernacular requires razor-sharp, rectilinear geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corners**: Strictly 90-degree corners across all structural containers, modals, buttons, form inputs, and image frames. This evokes stone slabs, structural steel framing, and planar glass facades.
- **Micro-accents**: Circular geometry is permitted solely for functional markers (e.g., active status indicators, compass orientators, playback scrub heads) where a `50%` radius differentiates a focal tool from the architecture.
- **Dividers & Lines**: Hairline profiles (`1px`), either solid at low opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) or broken with structural dashed treatments for technical floorplans and measurement axes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Inquire / Schedule Private Viewing)**: Sharp rectangular profile. Background of warm glowing amber (`#F2B36B`), text in pure dark obsidian (`#0F0F0F`), typography set to `label-md` uppercase. Hover state deepens the fill to an amber radiance (`#DDA058`) with a subtle outward halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(242, 179, 107, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Action**: Obsidian glass fill (`rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.5)`), bordered with hairline off-white (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Hover shifts border to amber (`rgba(242, 179, 107, 0.5)`) and text to `#F2B36B`.

### Cards & Property Modules
- Framed in frosted glass (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.6)`) with `1px` translucent borders.
- Imagery is treated with natural low-key contrast; on hover, images softly scale (`scale(1.02)`) over `700ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- Metadata overlays use `label-sm` tracking with amber bullet dividers.

### Chips & Floorplan Filters
- Sharp, understated pill-less tags. Rectangular bounds padded with `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally.
- Inactive: Transparent fill, bordered by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text in `#9E9E9E`.
- Active: Bordered by amber `#F2B36B`, text in `#FFFFFF`, with a delicate amber pip indicator (`3px` square) positioned left of the label.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural field aesthetic. Underline-only or full glass-trough design.
- Full Trough: `rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.8)` background, sharp edges, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Padding: `space-sm` by `space-md`. Text: `body-md` in `#FFFFFF`.
- Placeholder: `#9E9E9E` with slight letter tracking.
- Focus: Border transitions to `#F2B36B` with zero outline ring; an understated inner glow indicates presence.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkbox: Sharp `14px` square frame. Border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`. Checked state fills `#0F0F0F` with an amber interior glyph (`#F2B36B`) and amber perimeter line.
- Radio: Sharp `14px` diamond or concentric square frame. Active state displays an interior amber core square (`6px`).

### Lists & Specifications
- Divided by hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Two-column tabular design: left-aligned specification labels in `label-md` (`#9E9E9E`), right-aligned values in `body-md` (`#FFFFFF`).

### Architectural Plan / Gallery Viewer (Custom Component)
- Fullscreen immersive dark viewer equipped with hairline crosshairs, ambient amber focal points for room hotspots, and floating translucent glass control bars centered at the base.
